# Global LNG & Natural Gas Market Trends & Price Volatility Analytics

An end-to-end energy market data analytics project evaluating 10 years of historical natural gas spot prices (Henry Hub), U.S. LNG export throughput, and underground storage inventory cycles.

---

## 📌 Dataset Validation & Source

* **Source:** [U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA)](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/ng/ng_pri_fut_s1_m.htm)
* **Dataset:** Natural Gas Spot Prices, LNG Monthly Export Volumes, Underground Storage Reports
* **Period:** 2015 – 2024 (120 monthly market observations, official verified government energy data).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Programming & Modeling:** Python 3.10+ (Pandas, NumPy, Statsmodels, Matplotlib, Seaborn)
* **Relational Database & Querying:** SQL (PostgreSQL dialect) — Rolling Window Functions, YoY Growth Calculations
* **Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI (DAX measures, Seasonal trends, Arbitrage spread visuals)
* **Spreadsheet Modeling:** Advanced Microsoft Excel (Historical price modeling, seasonal variance)

---

## 📊 Key Findings

* **LNG Export Boom:** U.S. LNG export capacity grew over **400%** between 2016 and 2024, altering domestic storage injection cycles.
* **Price Dynamics:** Identified winter storage deficit as the strongest leading indicator for Q1 price volatility.
* **International Arbitrage:** Visualized global price differentials between domestic Henry Hub and European TTF benchmarks.
